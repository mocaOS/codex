# 3. Art Decc0s: The Collection

> The 10,000-piece collection — creative process, DNA system, art styles, philosophy, CC0 licensing.

---

## What Are Art Decc0s?

Art Decc0s are 10,000 unique digital characters that combine AI-generated silhouettes with masterworks from the MOCA collection. Each character is a composite of three layers:

- A **character silhouette** — a unique figure generated through a four-input DNA system that blends collector archetypes, crypto culture icons, artist self-portraits, and MOCA collection works
- A **background artwork** — drawn from sixteen art historical style categories spanning cave paintings to abstract expressionism
- A **complete identity** — tens of thousands of words of biographical, personality, and behavioral data stored in the Codex

### The Name

The name "Decc0s" fuses "Art Deco" aesthetics with "CC0" (Creative Commons Zero licensing), signaling both the artistic heritage and the radical openness of the project. The zero in "Decc0s" is a literal zero, not the letter O — a nod to the CC0 license that makes all artwork freely available.

## The Creative Process

### Nine Months of Intensive Work

The creative process behind Art Decc0s was a nine-month intensive effort led by MOCA, involving daily curation, character generation, background design, and content creation. Maxwell Cohen (lead writer) and DaïmAlYad (lead technical architect) drove the project through hundreds of iterative cycles.

The team identified three essential properties that every piece in the collection had to satisfy:

1. **Uniqueness** — Every combination of character and background had to be inimitable. No two pieces should feel like variations of each other.
2. **Diversity** — A truly dazzling array of aesthetics, with as much diversity as possible across styles, moods, and cultural references.
3. **Conceptual Coherence** — The aesthetics of any two pieces might at first appear antithetical, but the juxtaposition should ultimately feel like two answers to the same question.

The guiding philosophy balanced "aesthetic coherence and complete batshit depravity" — maintaining visual consistency while embracing crypto culture's surreal, experimental energy.

### Technical Workflow

The generation pipeline used several interconnected tools and processes:

**Core tools:**
- **ComfyUI** — A node-based interface for Stable Diffusion that served as the primary generation environment
- **Stable Diffusion** — The AI image generation model powering character and background creation
- **Python scripts** — Written with GPT-4 assistance by DaïmAlYad to automate assembly, matching, and renaming
- **Go programs** — Used for the Codex text generation pipeline

**Workflow parameters:**
The final workflow involved numerous interconnected settings, each requiring careful tuning:
- Model variants and checkpoint selection
- Noise parameters and seed control
- Shape consistency weights
- Efficiency measures for batch processing
- Input image selection and weighting
- Positive and negative prompting
- Upscaling and resolution management
- Background removal and compositing
- Auto-curation rules
- Iterative refinement based on curation feedback

**Hardware requirements:**
The project demanded substantial GPU power. Maxwell Cohen's MacBook Pro proved "borderline useless" for the aesthetic experimentation needed, necessitating well-endowed graphics cards for the image generation workloads.

**Assembly automation:**
DaïmAlYad wrote Python scripts that automated the final assembly:
1. Scripts matched generated DNA images to their source files using **pixel-level comparison**
2. Successfully matched outputs were renamed with DNA trait notation: `DNA1.DNA2.DNA3.DNA4_00006.png`
3. Character silhouettes were composited with backgrounds
4. Final images were curated, with rejected outputs fed back into the generation loop

### Iterative Refinement

Development involved continuous cycles: generating outputs, curating results, refining workflows based on curation, then regenerating. The process was not linear — it was a feedback loop that gradually converged on the desired aesthetic.

Early iterations produced undesirable results:
- "Muppet" characters with cartoonish, unnatural proportions
- Stilted symbology that felt forced rather than organic
- Backgrounds generating "noise" rather than coherent visual narratives

Success required stopping to conceptually interrogate every element, ensuring each aspect communicated meaning aligned with crypto art history. The team would pause generation runs to ask: "What is this element saying? Does it belong?"

## The DNA System

### Biological Metaphor

Rather than a traditional trait system used by most PFP projects (hat, shirt, eyes, background), Art Decc0s uses a biological metaphor. Four foundational input images serve as "DNA" — mirroring how adenine, cytosine, guanine, and thymine determine appearance in biology.

Each DNA strand contributes to the character's final visual form in unpredictable, emergent ways. Unlike traditional trait systems where a "red hat" always looks like a red hat, a DNA input might manifest as skin tone, clothing pattern, facial feature, accessory, or color palette depending on how it interacts with the other three inputs.

### DNA1 — Lineage (Collectors & Patrons)

Historical and cultural collector archetypes that acknowledge the role patrons have always played in shaping art movements:

- **Warlord** — Military rulers who commissioned art as displays of power
- **Sultan** — Islamic rulers whose courts produced some of history's greatest artistic achievements
- **Medici Family** — The Florentine banking dynasty that funded the Renaissance
- **Holy Roman Emperor** — European monarchs who built vast art collections as symbols of divine authority
- Additional archetypes including industrialists, bankers, and merchant princes

The patron is encoded into the art itself — a recognition that collectors don't just buy art, they shape it.

### DNA2 — Memetics (Crypto Culture Icons)

Both crypto-native memes and aesthetically memetic projects from the blockchain art world:

**Crypto memes:**
- Pepe
- Kevin
- Bitcoin Gasmask
- Wojak

**Memetic projects:**
- Chromie Squiggle (Art Blocks)
- Bored Apes (Yuga Labs)
- OPEPENs (Jack Butcher)
- CryptoPunks (Larva Labs)

The cultural DNA of the crypto movement becomes literal genetic material. A character with "Chromie Squiggle" in its DNA2 doesn't necessarily display a squiggle — the influence might appear as a color gradient in the skin, a pattern on clothing, or a subtle visual rhythm in the overall composition.

### DNA3 — Artist Self-Portraits

Classical masters and contemporary artists, establishing Art Decc0s within the tradition of identity interrogation through art:

**Classical masters:**
- Vincent van Gogh
- Frida Kahlo
- Pablo Picasso
- Jan van Eyck
- Rembrandt

**Contemporary artists:**
- Yayoi Kusama
- Zanele Muholi
- Cindy Sherman

This DNA strand connects each Art Decc0 to the centuries-long tradition of artists examining identity through self-portraiture.

### DNA4 — MOCA Collection Works

Seventy artworks by seventy different artists from MOCA's three collections:

- **Genesis Collection** — Early blockchain-based artworks
- **DaïmAlYad Collection** — Contemporary crypto art
- **Permanent Collection** — Works of lasting significance

The museum's holdings become part of each character's genetic makeup. This is not mere reference — the actual visual characteristics of these artworks influence how each character looks, creating a direct lineage between the crypto art movement's masterworks and the 10,000 characters that carry them forward.

### Emergent Aesthetics

Crucially, DNA traits do not reliably predict final aesthetics. This is a deliberate and philosophically significant design choice:

- A "Chromie Squiggle" DNA2 trait might manifest as skin tone in one character, clothing in another, or a crown in a third
- A "Medici" DNA1 trait could appear as posture, color palette, or facial expression
- Two characters sharing the same DNA combination can still look dramatically different

This unpredictability serves multiple purposes:
1. **Prevents rarity-based value hierarchies** — You can't predict "rare = beautiful"
2. **Forces organic discovery** — Collectors must engage with the art, not the metadata
3. **Creates genuine surprise** — Every reveal is meaningful
4. **Mirrors biology** — Just as identical twins can look different, shared DNA doesn't guarantee shared appearance

### The Form Trait

Beyond the four DNA strands, a "form" trait differentiates between:
- **9,500 standard characters** — Full silhouette figures with recognizable human forms
- **500 conceptual pieces** — More abstract, experimental interpretations that push the boundaries of what a "character" can be

Sixteen artistic style categories inform the backgrounds, each honoring a distinct art historical tradition. Every category required its own hand-curated training dataset and unique workflow with distinct positive/negative prompting and model tuning.

| 3 | **Eastern European Patterning** | Intricate geometric and folk patterns from Slavic, Baltic, and Balkan traditions |

| 8 | **Renaissance Painting** | The rebirth of classical ideals — perspective, naturalism, and humanist aspiration |
| 9 | **Medieval Painting** | Pre-Renaissance flatness, gold leaf, and religious symbolism — art as spiritual practice |
| 10 | **Japanese Woodblock Art** | Ukiyo-e and beyond — floating world aesthetics, nature studies, and the beauty of impermanence |
| 11 | **Worldwide Architecture** | Structural beauty from every continent — arches, domes, facades, and skylines |
| 12 | **Pop Art** | Mass culture as high art — bold colors, commercial imagery, and ironic commentary |

The tension between character and background is a core aesthetic feature. In some pieces, the relationship is "palpable" — a character and background that seem to resist each other. ## Philosophy & Conceptual Framework

Art Decc0s was designed to address five central questions about digital art, identity, and the PFP format. Each question reflects years of accumulated thinking about what crypto art should be.

### 1. Digital Identity Limits

**Question:** How closely can a single image represent the multifaceted nature of identity?

The project acknowledges the impossibility of total representation — no single image can capture a person's full complexity. But it argues the effort remains worthwhile. The solution embraced paradox: each character is so overwhelmingly detailed, so specifically characterized with biography, personality, preferences, and behavioral patterns, that "the sheer glut of characteristic individuality is itself unifying."

The Codex extends identity far beyond the visual. A character's "identity" includes not just how they look, but how they think, speak, feel, argue, create, and relate to the world.

### 2. Destroying Identity Politics

**Question:** Can a collection exist that resists the tokenization of specific identities?

The collection deliberately obscures quick racial and gender identification:
- Characters feature ambiguous physical traits
- Skin tones vary across a wide spectrum
- Masculine and feminine characteristics overlap and blend
- No character is easily categorizable

The technical implementation was equally intentional. Rather than asking a vision model once about gender identity, DaïmAlYad employed an iterative approach:
- Each character's masculinity was rated 8 times independently
- Each character's femininity was rated 8 times independently
- Results were averaged and weighed against each other
- This produced determinations more aligned with human perception than single-pass evaluations

The goal: collectors from any background can form personal connections with any piece, without the collection reducing identity to checkboxes.

### 3. Organic Value Creation

**Question:** Can value emerge organically in a PFP collection rather than being predetermined by rarity?

MOCA rejected the standard approach where rare traits automatically equal higher value and visual appeal. In most PFP projects, the rarity chart is the value chart — and everyone knows it at mint.

Art Decc0s deliberately decouples rarity from aesthetics:
- Common DNA traits produce wildly varied aesthetics
- Rare traits don't guarantee visual appeal
- Two characters sharing DNA can look completely different
- The community must discover value through engagement, not spreadsheets

This forces a different kind of collector behavior — one based on genuine aesthetic response rather than metadata speculation.

### 4. Reclaiming the PFP Medium

**Question:** Can the PFP format — historically associated with cash grabs — become a vehicle for genuine artistic expression?

MOCA directly addresses the criticism that selling NFTs contradicts their museum mission. They argue:
- Six months of intensive team labor justifies the sale
- Transparency about resource needs is more honest than pretending institutions don't need funding
- The PFP medium itself becomes tongue-in-cheek commentary: "reanimating a corpse and forcing it to sing"

By investing substantial creative labor into a format that most projects treat as minimal-effort profit extraction, MOCA proposes "a roadmap for the legitimization of PFPs." The message: this format can carry real artistic weight if creators take it seriously.

### 5. AI as Creative Influence vs. Theft

**Question:** Where is the line between AI-powered creative influence and appropriation?

The project uses AI generation trained on owned and referenced artworks. The team acknowledges borrowing from cultural touchstones like Bored Apes and Chromie Squiggles — icons they don't own — and asks whether this differs meaningfully from how traditional artists have always drawn on the visual culture around them.

The question is left deliberately unresolved. Art Decc0s doesn't claim to have the answer but positions itself as an honest participant in the conversation about AI's role in creative production.

## Identity & Representation

The strategic vision for Art Decc0s extends beyond static profile pictures. MOCA positions the characters as **visual identifiers for AI agents** — moving from profile pictures to "actors capable of action."

Each Art Decc0 can serve as:
- The **face** of an autonomous digital agent
- The **voice** defined by SOUL.md personality files
- The **persona** grounded in biography, cultural context, and behavioral rules
- The **identity** recognized across platforms and frameworks

This vision has already begun materializing through the DeCC0 Agent Launcher (powered by ElizaOS), the Adoption Center (conversational discovery), and the Codex API that makes all personality data programmatically accessible.

## Licensing: CC0

### What CC0 Means

All Art Decc0s artwork is released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)** — the most permissive possible license. CC0 is not merely an open license; it is a complete and irrevocable waiver of all copyright and related rights. This means:

| Permission | Status |
|------------|--------|
| Use for any purpose | Allowed |
| Modify and create derivatives | Allowed |
| Commercial use | Allowed |
| Attribution required | No |
| Share-alike required | No |
| Permission needed | No |

### Why CC0?

The choice of CC0 reflects MOCA's belief that:
- Culture grows through sharing, not artificial scarcity
- The value of the collection lies in its identity data and community, not in restricting access
- Derivative works strengthen the ecosystem rather than threatening it
- Open art invites the kind of creative experimentation that produced crypto art in the first place

### Open Source Code

All code in the Codex infrastructure is also open source:
- The Codex API backend: [github.com/mocaOS/codex](https://github.com/mocaOS/codex)
- The Codex Explorer frontend: [github.com/mocaOS/codex-explorer](https://github.com/mocaOS/codex-explorer)

This enables anyone to fork and adapt the entire system for other NFT collections — creating their own Codex with their own characters, data schema, and personality files.
